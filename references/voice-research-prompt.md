I'm launching a [PRODUCT TYPE] in the [PROBLEM/NICHE] space. Go to Reddit, Facebook groups, WebMD forums, health boards, Quora, and any other public discussion forums AND, in addition, the Japanese platforms: Yahoo!知恵袋, 5ちゃんねる, X (Twitter), 教えて!goo, 発言小町, Amebaブログ, note, and comments on Amazon/楽天 reviews. Find 40 real posts from people in our target demographic [TARGET DEMOGRAPHIC]; roughly half from English-language sources and half from Japanese sources talking in raw, unfiltered detail about the emotional pain, frustration, fear, or anxiety caused by [THE UNDERLYING PROBLEM] — not any specific product.
Prioritize posts that contain: rock-bottom moments, emotional tipping points, obsessive worries, shame spirals, relationship strain [NICHE-SPECIFIC EXAMPLES], moments they almost gave up, or the internal monologue they'd never say out loud. Skip posts where people are reviewing or complaining about a product. I want the PROBLEM, not the solution.
Pull the full conversations — the original post AND the replies that add emotional depth. Then analyze everything you found and give me:
Verbatim Language Bank — The exact phrases, words, metaphors, and slang they use, in the original language (English posts in English, Japanese posts in Japanese). Do not paraphrase or translate. Their words ARE my copy. Organize by: phrases describing the problem, phrases describing how it makes them feel, phrases describing what they've tried, phrases describing what they wish existed. Cite the source URL for each phrase.
Emotional State Map — What are the dominant emotional states across these posts? (shame, exhaustion, fear, anger, hopelessness, guilt, etc.) Rank by frequency.
Psychological Patterns — What beliefs do they hold about why the problem exists? Who/what do they blame? What have they tried and why do THEY think it failed? What tiny ember of hope keeps them searching?
Keyword & Jargon List — Every recurring term, acronym, or piece of insider language this demographic uses [SEED TERMS in both languages]. These are trust signals in copy.
The Sentence They'd Never Say Out Loud — Based on everything you've read, what is the deepest unspoken fear or shame running underneath all of these posts? Write it as a single sentence in their voice — give both an English version and a Japanese version.
This output feeds directly into writing persuasive direct response copy using Breakthrough Advertising (Schwartz) and RMBC method principles. Include everything that would help a copywriter channel this person's voice, match their awareness level, and write headlines that make them think "this person is inside my head." Keep all quotes in their original language; your analysis may be in English. Mark each quote with its source platform and country.
